Project 1 - Fraud and scams data model

The Fraud Operations team needs a trusted reporting model to monitor fraud and scam cases, 
measure financial loss, track detection effectiveness, and understand operational performance.

Currently, fraud data is spread across transactions, alerts, cases, customers, accounts, 
and rule outcomes, making it difficult to consistently answer questions about losses, 
false positives, case resolution times, and fraud trends.

The purpose of this data mart is to provide a clean, business-ready model 
that supports Power BI reporting on fraud cases, financial impact, detection outcomes, 
and operational performance.

Q1 and Q2:
Fact Table - Fact_Transaction
The Grain (What 1 row represents) - One row per transaction attempt.
Why I need it - To calculate total spend, fraud losses, and false-positive rates on rules.

Fact Table - Fact_Case
The Grain (What 1 row represents) - One row per fraud/scam investigation case.
Why I need it - 
To calculate operational metrics like case volume, resolution times, and analyst workloads.

Q4:
Dimension Table - Dim_Customer
What Data It Holds - Customer ID, Age Group, Signup Date, Risk Tier.
Why I need it - Shared dimension. Connects transactions to cases and allows you to see whether high-risk customer segments are being targeted by scammers.

Dimension Table - Dim_Account
What Data It Holds - Account ID, Account Type, Status.
Why I need it - Shared dimension. Helps track which account types are most vulnerable and measure the operational impact of freezing accounts.

Dimension Table - Dim_Date
What Data It Holds - Calendar Date, Month, Quarter, Year, Day of Week.
Why I need it - Shared dimension. Needed for Power BI time intelligence, tracking transaction trends over time, and measuring case resolution speed using opened and closed dates.

Dimension Table - Dim_Tran_Type
What Data It Holds - Transaction Type, such as Wire, ACH, ATM, or Card Purchase.
Why I need it - Transaction-only dimension. Helps identify which payment methods scammers prefer using to move or drain funds.

Dimension Table - Dim_Channel
What Data It Holds - Channel Name, such as Mobile App, Desktop Web, In-Branch, or Call Center.
Why I need it - Transaction-only dimension. Helps identify which transaction channels fraud is occurring through.

Dimension Table - Dim_Case_Status
What Data It Holds - Case Status, such as Open, Under Investigation, or Resolved.
Why I need it - Case-only dimension. Helps measure the fraud operations pipeline, backlog, and how many cases are still open.

Dimension Table - Dim_Scam_Type
What Data It Holds - Scam Category, such as Account Takeover, Romance Scam, or Investment Scam.
Why I need it - Case-only dimension. Helps understand fraud and scam trends because different scam types require different investigation approaches, controls, and customer education.

Dimension Table - Dim_Analyst
What Data It Holds - Analyst ID, Operations Team Name, Region.
Why I need it - Case-only dimension. Helps measure operational performance by tracking case distribution, analyst workload, and average resolution time by team or investigator.
